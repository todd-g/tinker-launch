Payments

Stripe + SafeSave. SafeSave is the markup vehicle — couldn't verify the exact spread above interchange, but operator quotes confirm it's a sore point. Convenience-fee pass-through to members is now in beta after years of operator requests.

The rent-extraction patterns you spotted — confirmed

  1. Per-court tier gating. Adding courts costs CourtReserve nothing in software/compute, but bumps you from $199 → $369 → $549. 9 courts = $170/mo more for one extra row in a database.
  2. Branded mobile app gated to top tier + $499 setup + 1-year contract. A white-label deliverable that's table stakes for serious clubs, walled off.
  3. $25/mo per integration. DUPR, Stringing, Video, Access Control. None of these cost CR meaningful marginal $ to maintain. Classic à la carte upsell stack.
  4. "Contact sales" wall the moment you exceed 32 courts or want multi-location. Opaque pricing is itself a tax on larger operators.
  5. No native website hosting. Operators must embed CR's widget into their own site. From Capterra: "Wish they would also be a website hosting."
  6. Payment processing markup via SafeSave. Operator on CR's own feedback board: "We are getting killed with credit card fees from Safesave. Please help us!"

Caveat The "no seasonal pause" angle you mentioned — I tried to verify it as a documented complaint and it was refuted by adversarial verification (1-2 vote). Operators complain about season-related workflows, but not specifically about being unable to pause billing. Still a likely opening, just not yet evidenced.

Disruption thesis

What a replacement targeting club operators should look like:

  1. Flat or transparent per-transaction pricing. Kill the court-count tier. Charge a flat SaaS fee regardless of court count, OR a flat % of bookings — the only metric that actually correlates with value delivered.
  2. Bundle the integrations. DUPR, video, access control, stringing — included. They cost you nothing meaningful to ship.
  3. Branded mobile app free on every tier. No setup fee, no contract lock-in. This is the loudest signal you're not them.
  4. Payment processing at cost. Pass Stripe through at 2.9% + 30¢, or negotiate Stripe Connect rates and split the savings. Native convenience-fee pass-through on day one.
  5. Native website hosting. Give clubs a real club site — branded, SEO'd — not just a widget to embed in a Squarespace.
  6. Seasonal pause built in. Outdoor/northern clubs should be able to pause billing 4-5 months a year. Huge lever for the seasonal segment even though it's not yet a top documented complaint.
  7. Fix the broken stuff on CR's own feedback board. Multi-player court-fee splitting, partial refunds, better mobile UX. Months of work, not years.
  8. Migration as a feature. "We migrate you in 7 days, including member data and Stripe handoff." Migration friction is currently the biggest reason operators stay.

Open questions worth answering before committing

Segment

Customer segmentation. Is the price-sensitive sweet spot the 10-20 court indoor/outdoor seasonal club? That's the segment getting hit hardest by court-count tiering + no seasonal pause.

Switching cost

Migration friction. What does "switch in 7 days" actually require? Data export from CR, member re-onboarding, Stripe handoff, mobile app re-download. Each is a real cost.
