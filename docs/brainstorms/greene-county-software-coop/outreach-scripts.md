# Outreach Scripts

Drop in the bracketed details; both are usable nearly verbatim.

## ☎️ Script 1 — USDA Rural Development NY State Office

**Call: (315) 477-6400** (Syracuse). Ask for the **Business Programs specialist who covers Greene
County**, or to ask RBDG eligibility questions.

**Open with:**
> "Hi, my name is Todd Galloway — I'm in Greene County. I'm exploring a Rural Business Development
> Grant to fund a project that would research and pilot a shared, **at-cost software service for
> local small businesses** — helping them cut what they spend on out-of-county software
> subscriptions. Before I invest in an application, I'd like to confirm eligibility. Could I ask a
> few questions or be routed to the right specialist?"

**Questions to get answered:**
1. **Project fit** — Does a project to do **market research, a feasibility study, and a small pilot**
   of a shared software service for rural small businesses qualify? Is it an **Opportunity** grant,
   an **Enterprise** grant, or could it be either?
2. **Applicant** — I have two possible applicants: (a) an existing **501(c)(3) that already does
   fiscal sponsorship**, and (b) the **Greene County IDA/EDC**. Would both qualify? Which makes the
   **stronger** application for this kind of project?
3. **Pass-through rule** — How should I scope it so it clearly benefits **many** businesses and isn't
   treated as a "pass-through" to a single business?
4. **Eligible costs** — Can funds cover the survey, **consultant/staff time**, and a prototype/pilot?
   Anything that's *not* eligible?
5. **Mechanics** — Confirm there's **no match** required and that it's **reimbursement-based** — any
   cash-flow I should plan for?
6. **Size** — What's a realistic **grant size in NY** for a feasibility/planning project, and what's
   this year's **Opportunity-grant cap**?
7. **Timing** — The current window closed June 30 — when does the **next NOFO post**, and what's the
   **FY2027 deadline**? Can I start a **pre-application conversation now**?
8. **Process** — Is there a **pre-application or letter-of-intent** step? Can you send the
   **application toolkit/checklist**, and who's my **Area Office contact for Greene County**?

**Close with:**
> "Thank you — could I get your name and email, and the best Greene County contact? Please send the
> application toolkit; I'll stay in touch as I line up an applicant."

## ✉️ Script 2 — Greene County IDA / EDC

*(Email first; the talking points double as a call agenda. Attach the concept memo.)*

**Subject:** Economic-development idea for Greene County — keeping local software dollars local

> Dear [Name / Greene County IDA / EDC team],
>
> My name is Todd Galloway, a [Greene County resident / business owner in ___]. I'm developing an
> economic-development project that I believe sits squarely in your mission, and I'd value 20–30
> minutes of your time.
>
> **The short version:** Greene County's small businesses send money out of the county every month to
> national software vendors — Square, Toast, Squarespace, QuickBooks, and dozens more. I want to
> start a **member-owned, nonprofit cooperative** that hosts proven open-source versions of those
> tools locally and offers them to rooted Greene County businesses **at cost** — keeping that
> recurring spend in the county. It serves the kind of businesses that employ local people; it
> explicitly does *not* serve absentee operators.
>
> I'm reaching out because the grants that could fund this — the **NY Consolidated Funding
> Application** (including ESD's feasibility-study program) and the **USDA Rural Business Development
> Grant** — require an eligible applicant such as an **IDA, LDC, county, or nonprofit**. I'd like to
> explore whether [the IDA / EDC] would consider being the **applicant or co-applicant** on a first,
> **feasibility-phase** grant: a survey to quantify the dollars leaving the county, followed by a
> small pilot.
>
> **A few things I'd love to discuss:**
> - Is [the IDA/EDC] already working on anything in **small-business digital support** I should align
>   with rather than duplicate?
> - Would you consider **sponsoring or co-applying** on a CFA and/or USDA RBDG application?
> - What's your **process and timeline** to authorize a grant application?
> - The **2026 CFA is open now** with a deadline around **late July** — any appetite to pursue it this
>   cycle, or should we position for the next round?
> - Could you help connect me with the **Chamber** and the **Capital Region SBDC**?
>
> I've attached a one-page concept memo. Could we find a short time to talk in the next week or two?
>
> Thank you,
> **Todd Galloway** — [email] · [phone]
