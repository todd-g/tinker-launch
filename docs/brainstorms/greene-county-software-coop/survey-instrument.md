# Business Survey Instrument

*The questions that produce the dollars-out number. ~8–10 minutes. Designed to (a) measure money
leaving the county, (b) test willingness to switch, (c) capture warm leads.*

## Section 1 — About your business
1. Business name and town (Catskill, Windham, Coxsackie, etc.)
2. Industry (restaurant / lodging / retail / trades / personal services / professional / other)
3. Number of employees: ☐ Just me ☐ 2–4 ☐ 5–9 ☐ 10–19 ☐ 20+
4. Years in business · Is the owner local and on-site? ☐ Yes ☐ No

5. **Roughly, what do you pay for ALL business software per month, total?**
   ☐ <$50 ☐ $50–150 ☐ $150–400 ☐ $400–1,000 ☐ $1,000+

## Section 3 — Pain & willingness
6. Which of these is most frustrating or most expensive? *(open)*
7. If a **locally-owned, member co-op** offered the same tools **at cost**, with **in-person setup
   help**, how likely would you be to switch? ☐ Very ☐ Somewhat ☐ Unsure ☐ Unlikely
8. What's the #1 thing that would make you switch? What's the #1 thing that would stop you? *(open)*
9. How comfortable are you setting up new software yourself?
   ☐ Very ☐ Somewhat ☐ I need help / hire it out
10. Would knowing your money stays in Greene County factor into your choice?
    ☐ A lot ☐ Some ☐ Not really

## Section 4 — Stay involved *(the warm-lead capture)*
11. Can we follow up with a 20-minute conversation? ☐ Yes (name, email, phone) ☐ Not now
12. Would you consider being an early pilot business? ☐ Yes ☐ Maybe ☐ No
