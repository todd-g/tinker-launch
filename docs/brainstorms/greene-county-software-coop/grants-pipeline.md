## USDA RBDG — detail

A grant to fund a **project that benefits rural small businesses**, awarded to an eligible *entity*
— **not** a grant to a business. **No matching funds required.** Disbursed as **reimbursements**
(applicant fronts costs). Beneficiary businesses must have **<50 employees and <$1M revenue**.

**Two types — the survey/feasibility phase fits both:**
- **Opportunity grant** — tech-based economic development, **feasibility studies, business plans,
  long-term strategic planning**, business support centers. Clean fit. *(Capped at 10% of the annual pool.)*
- **Enterprise grant** — TA, project planning, business counseling, **market research, feasibility
  studies**, plus capital (equipment, buildings, revolving loan funds).

**The "pass-through" rule (important):** USDA guards against the grantee being a conduit that funnels
funds to a single private business. The project must be scoped and run as a genuine **multi-business
community benefit** — which the co-op feasibility work naturally is. "Fund our startup" would not qualify.

## Applicant options compared

| Applicant | Strength | Watch-out | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Greene County IDA / EDC** | Built for this; grant-savvy; reviewers love it; reinforces "community benefit" framing | Slower (board/legislature authorization); you must sell them | **Strongest** for both CFA *and* RBDG |
| **Existing fiscal-sponsor nonprofit** (e.g. wife's) | Existing relationship; possibly faster yes; more control | Federal-compliance capacity? Willing? Must *own* the project (not pass-through) | Viable Plan B or **co-applicant** |
| **Form your own 501c3 / co-op** | Full control; permanent vehicle | Months to stand up; no track record yet | The long-term home, not the first app |

These are **not mutually exclusive** — strong applications often pair an established econ-dev entity
*with* a nonprofit and the Chamber as named partners.

> **Core principle:** you don't apply as yourself. An eligible entity applies as legal "grantee,"
> holds the money and the compliance burden, and your project runs underneath via an MOU/contract.
